Delete your account

Calkey — AI calorie tracker · Interludeapps

Calkey needs a Google sign-in, and keeps a backup of your journal with that account: your name and email address, an internal account identifier, the meals you logged (the text you typed and the calorie and macronutrient figures), water and weight entries, your goals and body details, your partner link if you have one, and your subscription status. Deleting your account removes all of it.

Delete it from inside the app

This is the quickest way, and it takes effect immediately.

  1. Open Calkey.
  2. Tap the settings icon at the top right of the journal.
  3. Scroll to Account and tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm. Your account and everything in it is removed from our servers and from the phone, and any partner link ends. This cannot be undone.

If you can't open the app

Write to support@calkey.app from the email address you signed in with, and say you want your Calkey account deleted. We reply within 7 days and complete deletion within 30. We delete exactly what the in-app button deletes.

What is deleted, and what is not

DeletedKept
Your account (name, email, identifier), journal, water and weight entries, goals, body details, partner link and daily summaries, and subscription status Per-request technical records (when a meal was parsed, how long it took), which never contained your meal text and lose any link to you; and anonymous tallies that never contained your identity

Your subscription

Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription, because Google bills it, not us. Cancel it first in Google Play → Profile → Payments and subscriptions → Subscriptions.
