Account deletion

Delete your account

Calkey — AI calorie trackerInterludeapps

Calkey needs a Google sign-in, and keeps a backup of your journal with that account: your name and email address, an internal account identifier, the meals you logged (the text you typed and the calorie and macronutrient figures), water and weight entries, your goals and body details, your partner link if you have one, and your subscription status. Deleting your account removes all of it.

01Delete it from inside the app

This is the quickest way, and it takes effect immediately.

  1. Open Calkey.
  2. Tap the settings icon at the top right of the journal.
  3. Tap your account card at the top, then Delete account.
  4. Confirm. Your account and your journal are removed from our servers and from the phone, and any partner link ends. This cannot be undone.

02If you can't open the app

Write to support@calkey.app from the email address you signed in with, and say you want your Calkey account deleted. We reply within 7 days and complete deletion within 30. We delete exactly what the in-app button deletes.

support@calkey.app

03What is deleted, and what is not

Deleted

Your account (name, email, identifier), journal, water and weight entries, goals, body details, partner link and daily summaries, and subscription status

Kept

Per-request technical records (when a meal was parsed, how long it took), which never contained your meal text and lose any link to you; anonymous tallies that never contained your identity; the shared caches of meal words and their figures, which never held a link to you; and a scrambled form of your Google account identifier, with whether its free day was used and whether it was blocked, so the same Google account cannot claim a second free day

04Your subscription

Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription, because Google bills it, not us. Cancel it first in Google Play → Profile → Payments and subscriptions → Subscriptions.
