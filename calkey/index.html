How it works

Three steps. The first one is typing.

01

Type it like a text

English, Hindi, Hinglish, Spanish, Bahasa — whatever you'd send a friend. Or say it.

02

See the reasoning

Each item, its portion and its calories, laid out line by line. Estimates are labelled as estimates.

03

Correct in two taps

Wrong portion? Tap it, pick another. Calkey remembers your usual for next time.

Honest numbers

Every figure says it's an estimate.

Open any meal and you see the items behind the total, how the figure was reached, and an amber flag on anything Calkey wasn't sure about. Change a portion and the number changes with it — and that becomes your default next time.

Questions

Before you ask.

When does it launch?

Android first, soon. Everyone on the waitlist gets an invite before the public launch, in the order they joined.

Is it on iPhone?

Not at launch. Calkey starts on Android. If you're on iPhone, plug your vote into the iPhone battery. Every vote moves iOS up our list.

Which languages and cuisines?

You can type in any language — the model reads it directly. US and Western food works out of the box, along with South Asian, Southeast Asian and Latin American home cooking, and we're adding regions based on what the waitlist eats. Pick yours when you sign up.

Do I have to weigh my food?

No. Say "a cup", "2 slices", "1 katori" — whatever you'd normally say. Close enough every day beats perfect for a week.

What can my partner see?

Only today's totals: calories against the goal, protein, carbs and fat, water, and the streak. Never the meals or the words you typed. You pair with a one-time code, one person at a time, and either of you can stop sharing whenever you like.

What will it cost?

Your first day is free, no card needed. After that Calkey is a subscription — yearly or monthly, starting with a 3-day free trial through Google Play. Everyone on the waitlist gets a mystery discount on their first month, revealed at launch.

What do you do with my email?

Send you a launch invite and the occasional update. Never sold, never shared. Reply to any email to be removed.

Is this medical advice?

No. Calkey gives estimates to help you track — it doesn't diagnose or treat anything. Talk to a doctor or dietitian for medical guidance.
