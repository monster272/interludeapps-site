01Partner sharing

Share today with one person.

Pair with a partner, a friend or a trainer and you each see the other's day under your own: calories against the goal, protein, carbs, fat, water and the streak. Never what either of you ate.

01 · PAIR

A one-time code. That's the whole setup.

One of you makes a code in Settings › Partner, the other types it in. It works once and expires in 48 hours. One person at a time, nobody can find you, and there's nothing to follow.

02 · LIVE

See each other's day as it happens.

Log a meal and your partner's card updates within seconds, right under their own totals in the today sheet. No refreshing, no feed to scroll.

03 · CHEER

Hear about the moments that matter.

A notification when your partner hits the protein goal, lands right on target, or reaches a streak milestone. Goal moments only, never one per meal, each at most once a day, and quiet from 10 pm to 8 am.

04 · PRIVATE

Totals travel. Meals stay with you.

  • Today's calories, against the goal
  • Protein, carbs and fat
  • Water and the streak
  • Never what you ate, or anything you typed
  • No history, no feed, no followers

Either of you can stop sharing at any time, and it stops for both.

02Measures that know where you live

A cup isn't a cup everywhere.

Say "a cup of chai" in Pune and you mean a 150 ml chai cup. In São Paulo a xícara is 200 ml, and in Ohio a cup is 240. Calkey reads plain words the way your country does, so you never convert anything.

Local words work everywhere.

Every kitchen's measures ship to everyone, so "1 katori dal" is 150 g in Ohio too, and "uma concha de feijão" means a bean ladle anywhere. Your country only settles the plain words.

Set once, kept for good. Calkey works out your country the first time you open it and keeps it, so a holiday doesn't change your measures. Name a size, like "US cup", "200 ml" or "100 g", and that wins.

Questions

Before you ask.

When does it launch?

Android first, soon. Everyone on the waitlist gets an invite before the public launch, in the order they joined.

Is it on iPhone?

Not at launch. Calkey starts on Android. If you're on iPhone, plug your vote into the iPhone battery. Every vote moves iOS up our list.

Which languages can I write in?

Any language you'd text in: English, Hindi, Hinglish, Spanish, Portuguese, Bahasa, Arabic and more, mixed however you like. The app itself is in English at launch.

How does it know my country's measures?

Calkey works out your country from your phone the first time you open it, and keeps that answer. It only decides what plain words like "cup" and "bowl" mean. Words such as katori, concha or centong mean the same thing everywhere, and a size you name, like "200 ml", always wins.

Do I have to weigh my food?

No. Say "a cup", "2 slices", "1 katori", whatever you'd normally say. If you do know the weight, type it and Calkey does the exact sum.

What can my partner see?

Only today's totals: calories against the goal, protein, carbs and fat, water and the streak. Never the meals or the words you typed. You pair with a one-time code, one person at a time, and either of you can stop sharing whenever you like. Notifications are for goal moments only, at most once a day each.

What will it cost?

Calkey is a subscription, yearly or monthly, starting with a 3-day free trial through Google Play. Everyone on the waitlist gets a mystery discount on their first month, revealed at launch.

What do you do with my email?

Send you a launch invite and the occasional update. Never sold, never shared. Reply to any email to be removed. The full detail is in the privacy policy.

Is this medical advice?

No. Calkey gives estimates to help you track. It doesn't diagnose or treat anything. Talk to a doctor or dietitian for medical guidance.
