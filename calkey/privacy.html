What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data, and we do not share it for advertising.
  • We do not build a profile of you for anyone else. The only person who can see any of your figures is a partner you chose to pair with, and only your daily totals.
  • We do not record your meal text in our technical logs, our food backlog or our correction tallies.
  • We do not use your journal to train our own models.
  • No notification ever contains what you, or your partner, ate.

Notifications

Calkey only sends notifications if you allow them when the app asks. Meal reminders are made on your phone itself. Everything else comes from our server: your partner's goal moments, notices about your subscription, occasional news from us, and one gentle nudge if you have not logged for a few days (never more than one a week). We hold them between 22:00 and 08:00 your time, except a payment problem, and send no more than three a day.

You can switch partner updates, news and the nudge off in Settings, and silence any kind in your phone's notification settings. Subscription notices cannot be switched off in the app, because they are about money you may be charged.

Sharing with a partner

Partner sharing is off unless you turn it on. To pair, one of you makes a six-character code in Settings → Partner and the other types it in; a code works once and expires after 48 hours. You can be paired with one person at a time.

Once paired, you each see the other's totals for the day: calories eaten and left against the goal, protein, carbs and fat, water, and the current streak. Neither of you can see what the other ate, the text of any entry, or any history beyond the last few days.

Your partner sees the name on your Google account (or the first part of your email address if it has no name), and can rename you on their own phone.

Either of you can stop at any time with Settings → Partner → Stop sharing. It ends sharing in both directions immediately. Deleting either account also ends it.

The pre-launch waitlist

If you join the waitlist on our website, we store your email address, and if you give them: what you mostly eat, your browser's language setting, the site or link you came from, and whether you voted for an iPhone version. We use them to send you one email when the app is ready for you, to honour the first-month discount promised to everyone on the list, and, in aggregate, to decide which cuisines and countries to support first.

The list is not shared, sold or used for advertising. The page shows only how many people have joined. To be removed, write to support@calkey.app. We delete the whole list within 90 days of the app's public launch, once the launch invites and discounts have gone out.

Who else processes your data

  • Google — sign-in, the language model that reads your meal text, Google Play, which takes subscription payments and tells us whether a subscription is active, Firebase Cloud Messaging, which delivers notifications to your phone, Firebase Crashlytics, which receives crash reports, and Health Connect on your phone, if you switch it on, for water only.
  • Supabase — the database and server that hold your backup, and the waitlist.
  • Resend — delivers the messages you send us from the app to our support inbox. It receives the message, your email address and the app details listed above.
  • Exa — web search, for foods our tables do not cover. It receives a search query derived from your meal text, not your identity.

How long we keep it

Your journal, goals, partner link and subscription status are kept until you delete them or delete your account, and are removed with it. Our per-request technical records (when a meal was parsed, how long it took, whether it failed; never the meal text) lose any link to you when your account is deleted. Messages you send us from the app are deleted from our database after 90 days, or with your account; the copy in our support inbox is kept while we need it to help you. The anonymous tallies of corrections and unrecognised food words never contained your identity, and are kept to improve accuracy.

Deleting your data

In the app, open Settings → Delete account. This removes your account and everything in it, on the phone and on our servers, ends any partner link, and cannot be undone. It does not cancel a Google Play subscription, which Google bills, not us: cancel it first in Google Play → Subscriptions. If you can no longer open the app, follow the steps on our account deletion page or write to support@calkey.app; we reply within 7 days and complete deletion within 30.

Children

Calkey is meant for adults and is not directed at anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children; if you believe a child has created an account, write to us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, and to object to some processing. Write to support@calkey.app and we will respond within 30 days.

Not medical advice

Calkey gives estimates. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, and nothing in it should be used as a substitute for a qualified professional.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top of this page, and say so in the app when the change is significant.
