03What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data, and we do not share it for advertising.
  • We do not build a profile of you for anyone else. The only person who can see any of your figures is a partner you chose to pair with, and only your daily totals.
  • We do not record your meal text in our technical logs, our food backlog or our correction tallies.
  • We do not use your journal to train our own models.
  • No notification ever contains what you, or your partner, ate.

04Notifications

Calkey only sends notifications if you allow them when the app asks. Meal reminders are made on your phone itself. Everything else comes from our server: your partner's goal moments, notices about your subscription, occasional news from us, and one gentle nudge if you have not logged for a few days (never more than one a week). We hold them between 22:00 and 08:00 your time, except a payment problem, and send no more than three a day.

You can switch partner updates, news and the nudge off in Settings, and silence any kind in your phone's notification settings. Subscription notices cannot be switched off in the app, because they are about money you may be charged.

05Sharing with a partner

Partner sharing is off unless you turn it on. To pair, one of you makes a six-character code in Settings → Partner and the other types it in; a code works once and expires after 48 hours. You can be paired with one person at a time.

Once paired, you each see the other's totals for the day: calories eaten and left against the goal, protein, carbs and fat, water, and the current streak. Neither of you can see what the other ate, the text of any entry, or any history beyond the last few days.

Your partner sees the name on your Google account (or the first part of your email address if it has no name), and can rename you on their own phone.

Either of you can stop at any time with Settings → Partner → Stop sharing. It ends sharing in both directions immediately. Deleting either account also ends it.

06The pre-launch waitlist

If you join the waitlist on our website, we store your email address, and if you give them: what you mostly eat, your browser's language setting, the site or link you came from, and whether you voted for an iPhone version. We use them to send you one email when the app is ready for you, to honour the first-month discount promised to everyone on the list, and, in aggregate, to decide which cuisines and countries to support first.

When you join, we first send one email with a link, to check the address is yours. Until you tap it you are not on the list and are not counted; if you never tap it, we delete the address 7 days after that email. To stop automated sign-ups we also keep, for at most two days, a count of sign-ups per connection, keyed by a scrambled form of your IP address that changes daily and cannot be turned back into the address.

The list is not shared, sold or used for advertising. The page shows only how many people have confirmed. To be removed, write to support@calkey.app. We delete the whole list within 90 days of the app's public launch, once the launch invites and discounts have gone out.

07Who else processes your data

  • GoogleSign-in, the language model that reads your meal text, Google Play, which takes subscription payments and tells us whether a subscription is active, Firebase Cloud Messaging, which delivers notifications to your phone, Firebase Crashlytics, which receives crash reports, Play Integrity, which checks that the copy of Calkey talking to our server came from Google Play, and Health Connect on your phone, if you switch it on, for water only.
  • SupabaseThe database and server that hold your backup, and the waitlist.
  • PostHogReceives the anonymous usage statistics described above, if "Share usage statistics" is on. It never receives your meals, figures or identity.
  • ExaWeb search, for foods our tables do not cover. It receives a search query derived from your meal text, not your identity.
  • ResendDelivers the messages you send us from the app to our support inbox, our replies from support@calkey.app, and the waitlist's confirmation email and launch invite. It receives the message, your email address and, for messages from the app, the app details listed above.
  • ImprovMXForwards email you send to an @calkey.app address, such as support@calkey.app, to our support inbox. It receives that email.

08How long we keep it

Your journal, goals, partner link and subscription status are kept until you delete them or delete your account, and are removed with it. Our per-request technical records (when a meal was parsed, how long it took, whether it failed; never the meal text) lose any link to you when your account is deleted. Messages you send us from the app are deleted from our database after 90 days, or with your account; the copy in our support inbox is kept while we need it to help you. The anonymous tallies of corrections and unrecognised food words never contained your identity, and are kept to improve accuracy. The shared caches of meal words and their figures never held a link to you, and are kept with no set end date. Usage statistics are kept by PostHog for up to a year and are not linked to your account.

When an account is deleted we keep one thing: a scrambled form of its Google account identifier that names no one, with whether its free day was used and whether it was blocked, so the same Google account cannot claim a second free day. It is kept with no set end date.

09Deleting your data

In the app, open Settings → Account → Delete account. This removes your account and everything in it, on the phone and on our servers, except the scrambled identifier described above, ends any partner link, and cannot be undone. It does not cancel a Google Play subscription, which Google bills, not us: cancel it first in Google Play → Subscriptions.

If you can no longer open the app, follow the steps on our account deletion page or write to support@calkey.app; we reply within 7 days and complete deletion within 30.

10Children

Calkey is meant for adults and is not directed at anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children; if you believe a child has created an account, write to us and we will delete it.

11Your rights
